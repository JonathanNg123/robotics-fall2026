Respond to any or all of these prompts:

2. How did this activity affect your motivation to do similar kinds of work in the future?
3. What value do you see in connecting technical or computing work with human, ethical, or societal considerations?

## Response

This activity helped me understand that robotics involves much more than just writing code that works for a robot. The introduction of frames took me off guard as I had never heard of them and did not know they were required. The most interesting part for me was programming the robot to follow a specific motion pattern and then comparing the expected movement with what actually happened in the simulation. I learned that even when the calculations and automated tests are correct, the real or simulated robot may not behave exactly as expected. I had thought robots were perfect machines that can do whatever it is programmed to do. However, that is simply not the case as there is a lot of trial and error. This made me realize why testing and verification are so important in robotics.
